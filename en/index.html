CASE 01
ISO 9001 Nonconformance Report (NCR)
GENERAL AI OUTPUT
Write an ISO 9001 nonconformance report
Abstract
Work procedures were not followed. We will investigate the cause and prevent recurrence.
DOMAIN EXPERTISE × AI
During an overseas hydropower project, the wicket gate throttle bore diameter was smaller than drawing requirements. Assess the schedule impact and process this as an NCR.
Practical-grade
During inspection of the wicket gate assembly, it was identified that the throttle bore diameter was smaller than the value specified on drawing XXX. Engineering assessment confirmed that the deviation has no adverse effect on hydraulic performance, mechanical integrity, or operating reliability.
CASE 02
Technical Report (Hydropower — Where Expertise Stands Out Most)
GENERAL AI OUTPUT
Write about cavitation
Superficial
Cavitation occurred. Countermeasures are required.
DOMAIN EXPERTISE × AI
Calculate the cavitation margin at minimum tailwater level and evaluate countermeasures for draft tube pressure pulsation.
Hydropower engineer level
The calculated cavitation margin at minimum tailwater level is approximately 4 m above the admissible limit. Nevertheless, central air admission is recommended to suppress draft tube pressure pulsations and mitigate vortex rope development.
CASE 03
Construction Procedure (Generator Installation)
GENERAL AI OUTPUT
Write a generator installation procedure
Too simplistic
Install the generator. Tighten the bolts.
DOMAIN EXPERTISE × AI
Create an installation protocol for a vertical generator, including rotor lowering sequence, coupling bolt-hole alignment verification, and TIR tolerance.
Expert-level writing
Rotor shall be lowered in increments of approximately 10 mm. Alignment of coupling bolt holes shall be verified at each step. Runout shall not exceed 0.15 mm TIR at the turbine guide bearing.

International-project-ready
During the assembly verification process, an unexpected dimensional deviation was identified on the operating ring interface. To ensure full compliance with contractual and quality requirements, additional inspection activities have been initiated. The current schedule impact is estimated to be two working days.
